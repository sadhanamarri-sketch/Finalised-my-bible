# Verse Scroll's bundled data

Everything under `app/src/main/assets/verse_scroll/` is generated by these scripts (Python 3 with
numpy and Pillow):

- `scenes/*.webp`: the background photos, free photos from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/license)
  listed by kind and photo id in `photos.tsv`, cut to a phone screen by `fetch_photos.py`.
- `scenes.json`: for each photo, what it shows (`kind`, which verses are matched to) and how much it
  is dimmed under the text in dark themes (`kd`) and light ones (`kl`).
- `discover.tsv`: the verses Discover deals from, with their weights and tiers.

To regenerate:

```sh
python3 fetch_photos.py /tmp/photos /tmp/photo-cache      # downloads, crops and compresses the photos
python3 fit_veils.py /tmp/photos json > /tmp/veils.json   # measures the dimming each photo needs
python3 check_veils.py /tmp/photos /tmp/veils.json        # lowest contrast per app theme, for the record
python3 make_assets.py eng-kjv.osis.xml cross_references.txt /tmp/photos /tmp/veils.json \
    ../../app/src/main/assets/verse_scroll
```

`eng-kjv.osis.xml` and `cross_references.txt` are the same files the app downloads (see
`KjvImporter` and `CrossReferenceImporter`).

The dimming targets: the verse stands out by at least 4.5:1 and the reference by 3:1, measured over
the worst 3% of pixels anywhere text can sit. With motion on, the app adds a small margin
(`SceneLayer.drawVeil`) to cover the brighter patches the slow zoom can bring behind the text.
